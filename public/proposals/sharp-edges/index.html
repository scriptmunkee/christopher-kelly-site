    Can you send your knives to get sharpened?
    Yes. You can send your knives to us to get sharpened. Buy a prepaid USPS label on this page, pack each knife separately and drop the box at any Philadelphia post office. We sharpen within 1 to 3 business days and post them back.
    Do I need to sharpen my knives?
    Sharpen your knives regularly for better performance and safety. Sharp blades require less force and cut cleanly. Dull blades need extra pressure, increasing accident risk. Sharpening maintains edges worn down by use, and well-sharpened knives improve cooking and taste.
    Is it worth sharpening cheap knives?
    Affordable knives can be just as good as pricier options. Many cheap knives use high-quality steel similar to expensive brands, but cost less due to mass production.
    What is the going rate for knife sharpening?
    We charge $8 per knife up to 12 inches and $10 for longer knives, and you can check every rate in our mail-in sharpening price list.
    How does the prepaid USPS label work?
    Tell us how many knives and shears you're sending and your ZIP code. We quote the USPS postage from your address to our workshop, you pay online, and the label is emailed to you straight away to print and stick on your box.
    What if I don't have a printer?
    Choose the QR code option when you pay. Take your sealed box to a Post Office that offers USPS Label Broker, show the code on your phone, and the clerk prints your prepaid USPS label and sticks it on for you.
    How much does return shipping cost?
    Return shipping is free on sharpening orders over $100. Below that it's a flat $10, added when we invoice your sharpening.

    Live build: request flow
    1. Customer enters counts + ZIPPlugin turns counts into a parcel (box + weight) and calls the Shippo Rates API from the customer's ZIP to 73110. Rates are cached for 15 minutes.
    2. Add to cartA "Mail-In Postage Label" product is added with the chosen rate, item counts and parcel stored on the cart item. Normal WooCommerce checkout with the existing WooPayments and PayPal.
    3. Payment completePlugin re-quotes, buys the USPS label from Shippo, saves label PDF, tracking number and cost to the order, adds an order note.
    4. Customer emailLabel PDF attached to the order email; download link on the thank-you page and in My Account.
    5. TrackingShippo webhook moves the order to "Tools in transit", then "Received" when USPS delivers to the workshop.
    6. Sharpening invoiceSharpening lines added after inspection; return shipping rule ($10, free over $100) applied automatically.
