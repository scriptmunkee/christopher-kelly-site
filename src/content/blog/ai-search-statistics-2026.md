AI assistants have moved from novelty to shortlist. Customers ask ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Copilot and Google AI Mode which brand to choose, and many act on the answer before they visit a website. The numbers below show how fast that has happened, how well the traffic converts, where AI gets its answers and how often it gets them wrong.

Every figure links to its source. Where a study comes from a company that sells AI visibility, analytics or marketing services, I say so, because those studies are useful but not independent. Where the data is from the US or a global sample rather than the UK, I say that too.

## How Many People Use AI to Choose

Adoption is no longer a niche behaviour. It shows up in local services, shopping, money, health, law and B2B software.

| Statistic | What it measures | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 45% | US consumers who use ChatGPT and other AI tools for local recommendations, up from 6% a year earlier | [BrightLocal, Feb 2026](https://www.brightlocal.com/research/local-consumer-review-survey/) |
| 46% | UK online shoppers who have used AI tools while shopping; 19% use them every day | [Idealo survey via IT Brief UK, Aug 2026](https://itbrief.co.uk/story/uk-shoppers-embrace-ai-but-trust-remains-a-hurdle) (vendor) |
| 49% | Consumers worldwide who used AI to support savings and investment decisions in the past six months | [EY, Apr 2026](https://ey.com/en_gl/newsroom/2026/04/nearly-half-of-global-consumers-now-use-ai-to-guide-savings-and-investment-decisions) |
| 68% | Consumers across six markets, including the UK, who used AI assistants when buying insurance | [Geneva Association, Nov 2025](https://www.genevaassociation.org/publication/digital-ai-transformation/gen-ai-insurance-customer-journey) |
| 1 in 6 | People facing a legal dispute who have asked an AI chatbot for help | [JUSTICE, Sept 2026](https://www.resultsense.com/news/2026-09-17-justice-ai-chatbots-legal-disputes/) |
| 1 in 7 | UK adults who have used AI chatbots for health advice instead of contacting a GP or the NHS | [King's College London, May 2026](https://www.doctors.net.uk/news/one-in-seven-use-chatbots-instead-of-contacting-gp) |
| 54% | B2B decision-makers who rank AI chatbots as the top source shaping their software shortlist | [G2, Apr 2026](https://learn.g2.com/g2-2026-ai-search-insight-report) (vendor) |

Two of these stand out. The jump from 6% to 45% in BrightLocal's local data happened in a single year. And in B2B software, G2 found 69% of buyers chose a different vendor than they had planned because of a chatbot. AI is not just answering questions; it is changing who gets picked.

AI is also becoming a first stop rather than a last resort. [YouGov found](https://yougov.com/en-gb/articles/55203-ai-is-becoming-part-of-uk-adults-health-information-journey) that 8% of UK adults now go to AI tools first for health information, rising to 13% of Millennials.

## Does AI Traffic Convert?

For a long time the argument against AI search was that it sent too little traffic to matter. The volume is still smaller than Google, but the quality is high and growing fast.

- **Retail:** AI referrals to US retail sites converted 60% better than other traffic in July 2026, earned 53% more revenue per visit and grew 62% year on year, according to [Adobe data reported by Digital Commerce 360](https://www.digitalcommerce360.com/2026/08/19/adobe-ai-referral-traffic-data-july-2026/). Adobe sells analytics software.
- **B2B:** Monthly ChatGPT referral visits to B2B websites rose 303% in a year, to about 2.6 million in June 2026, according to [Demandbase](https://www.demandbase.com/press-release/chatgpt-b2b-referrals-growth/), an account-based marketing platform.
- **The catch:** Adobe also found about a third of product pages on US retail sites cannot be properly accessed by AI ([TechCrunch, Apr 2026](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/16/ai-traffic-to-us-retailers-rose-393-in-q1-and-its-boosting-their-revenue-too/)). Traffic you can't be recommended for never arrives.

## Where AI Gets Its Answers

If AI is choosing, the next question is what it reads. The evidence points consistently at brands' own sites and listings, with independent sources filling the gaps.

| Finding | Source |
|---|---|
| Most AI citations come from sources brands control: their websites and their listings | [Yext, Oct 2025](https://yext.com/about/news-media/ai-citations-release) (vendor) |
| Firm websites earned 74% of citations in UK solicitor answers on ChatGPT and Perplexity | [TendorAI, Jul 2026](https://www.tendorai.com/resources/ai-visibility-report-solicitors-july-2026) (vendor) |
| 53% of AI Overview citations for US insurance searches came from pages outside the organic top 10 | [Conductor, Aug 2026](https://www.conductor.com/academy/2026-insurance-ai-search-benchmarks/) (vendor) |
| In 2,034 fintech AI answers, Forbes was cited more often than any brand's own website | [5WPR via Everything-PR, Oct 2026](https://everything-pr.com/fintech-ai-visibility-findings) (vendor) |
| Branded web mentions were far more closely linked to AI visibility than domain strength, across 75,000 brands | [Ahrefs, 2025 to 2026](https://ahrefs.com/de/blog/ai-brand-visibility-correlations/) (vendor) |

Read together, these say two things. First, your own pages matter more than ever, because AI quotes them directly. Second, ranking well is no longer enough: Conductor's data shows AI happily cites pages that don't rank, and the fintech study shows a publisher can be quoted ahead of the brand itself when the brand's page is vague.

## How Often AI Gets It Wrong

AI answers are confident, but not always right. Local business details are where the errors are easiest to measure.

- Non-Google AI assistants were only about **68% accurate** on business contact details in [SOCi's 2026 Local Visibility Index](https://golocal.soci.ai/rs/355-TUF-572/images/The_2026_Local_Visibility_Index_SOCi100.pdf).
- ChatGPT's details matched Google Business Profile for only **56% of local businesses**, and the phone number was wrong for **30%**, in a US study of 10,000 businesses by [Insites](https://insites.com/blog/does-chatgpt-hallucinate-local-businesses-the-data-on-ai-accuracy/). Insites notes that call-tracking numbers may inflate the phone errors.
- **28.5%** of businesses in Google's Local Pack didn't appear in AI Mode for the same query, in a [Local SEO Data study](https://martechseries.com/predictive-ai/ai-platforms-machine-learning/local-seo-data-study-finds-28-5-of-local-pack-businesses-are-invisible-in-google-ai-mode/) of 1,120 US queries.

The practical point: AI pieces your details together from many sources. When those sources disagree, it guesses, and a wrong phone number sends the customer somewhere else.

## Do People Trust AI Answers?

They use them, and they check them. That combination is why AI visibility and the rest of your reputation now have to line up.

- **94%** of technology buyers who use AI fact-check its answers at least some of the time ([TrustRadius, Jul 2026](https://hginsights.com/news/trustradius-2026-b2b-buying-disconnect-report-reveals-ai-has-changed-how-buyers-research-but-not-what-they-trust/), vendor).
- Only **2%** of US consumers would buy from an unfamiliar brand on an AI recommendation alone; **45%** Google the brand straight away ([Idea Grove, Apr 2026](https://www.citybiz.co/?p=833574), vendor).
- **28%** of consumers tried a new local business in the past six months because an AI tool recommended it, but only 5% go straight from an AI answer to buying, and 53% check Google or Bing first ([Yext, 2026](https://www.yext.com/resources/consumer-search-behaviors-full-report), vendor).
- **53%** of UK adults say they often see AI Overviews in Google results ([Ofcom, Dec 2025](https://www.ofcom.org.uk/media-use-and-attitudes/online-habits/from-apps-to-ai-search-how-the-uk-goes-online-in-2025)).
